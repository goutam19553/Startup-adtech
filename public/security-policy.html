Security Policy

We at The Ad Project take security seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure.

Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security issue, please email us at theadproject01@gmail.com.

In-Scope

  • Our production website: theadproject.in
  • Public APIs and endpoints we own
  • Vulnerabilities that can impact users, data, or backend systems

Out of Scope

  • Third-party services we use (e.g., Vercel, Supabase)
  • Denial of Service (DoS)
  • Social engineering attacks

Hall of Fame

If your report is valid and helpful, we'll list your name in our Hall of Fame.
